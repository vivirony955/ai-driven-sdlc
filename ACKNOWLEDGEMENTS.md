# Acknowledgements

This project stands on a great deal of generous, openly-shared work.

## The reviewers

The v1.0 manuscript went through a full editorial review and remediation pass
before release; that work (21 review items, 14 actionable fixes closed) is recorded
in [`archive/editorial-history/`](archive/editorial-history/). Thank you to the
reviewers and editors who sharpened the book.

## The running case study

*Atlas Learn* — the fictional EdTech company whose incidents anchor the examples
and appendices — is invented, but it is shaped by the real, hard-won lessons of
many engineering teams operating AI systems in production. Thank you to the
practitioners whose experiences inform it.

## Open standards & communities this repository is built on

The repository's structure and community practices follow widely-shared open-source
norms:

- [GitHub Open Source Guides](https://opensource.guide/)
- [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) — Code of Conduct
- [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) & [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/)
- [Citation File Format](https://citation-file-format.github.io/)
- [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/) & [UNESCO OER](https://www.unesco.org/en/open-educational-resources) — open licensing for education
- [OpenSSF](https://openssf.org/) and [CNCF](https://www.cncf.io/) — project-health and documentation guidance

## Open-source tooling

The book's formats and website are produced with:

- [pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) — EPUB/HTML conversion
- [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) — faithful PDF rendering
- [MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/) + [Material for MkDocs](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/) — the website
- [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/features/actions) — automation
- [markdownlint](https://github.com/DavidAnson/markdownlint), [cSpell](https://cspell.org/), [linkinator](https://github.com/JustinBeckwith/linkinator) — quality checks
