# Bibliography & further reading

A curated, **community-maintained** reading list that situates this book's
framework in the wider literature. It is intentionally small in v1.0 and grouped
by theme; **additions are welcome** (see [contributing](#contributing-a-reference)).

> Format for each entry: *Author(s). Title. Venue/Publisher, year. URL.*
> Keep entries to widely-available, durable sources. Mark anything paywalled.

## Software engineering foundations

- _Seed the list:_ classic texts on software lifecycle, design, and quality that
  the book's "engineering discipline first" stance builds on.

## LLM agents & AI-assisted development

- _Seed the list:_ surveys and primary sources on language-model agents, tool use,
  prompting, and evaluation.

## Responsible & ethical AI

- _Seed the list:_ frameworks and guidance referenced by the *Ethics & Responsible
  AI* bridge.

## Open educational resources (OER) & open licensing

- Creative Commons. *About CC Licenses.* https://creativecommons.org/about/cclicenses/
- UNESCO. *Recommendation on Open Educational Resources (OER).* 2019.

## Open-source project stewardship (used to build this repo)

- GitHub. *Open Source Guides.* https://opensource.guide/
- Contributor Covenant. https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
- *Keep a Changelog.* https://keepachangelog.com/
- *Semantic Versioning.* https://semver.org/
- *Citation File Format (CFF).* https://citation-file-format.github.io/
- OpenSSF. *Best Practices.* https://openssf.org/

## Contributing a reference

Open a pull request adding an entry under the right theme, in the format above.
Prefer primary sources and stable URLs; add `(accessed YYYY-MM-DD)` for anything
that may move. If you cite a source inside the manuscript, add it here too so the
two stay in sync.
