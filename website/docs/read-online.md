## Reader paths

A careful **linear** read works for everyone, but most readers do better with a
guided path. Whichever you choose: **read the Preface first and the Glossary last.**

| Path | For | Suggested order |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — First-year undergraduate** | Vocabulary & intuition | Ch 1 → 2 → 3 → 10 (roles) → 13 → 23 |
| **2 — Upper-level / new practitioner** | Add disciplines & quality | Parts I–IV linearly |
| **3 — Senior engineer / tech lead** | The full operating spine + bridges | Linear, all bridges |
| **4 — Self-learner / skeptic** | Motivation, limits, principles first | Preface → 1 → 22 → 23, then the middle |
